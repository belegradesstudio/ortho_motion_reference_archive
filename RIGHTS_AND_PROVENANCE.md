# Rights and provenance

The archive tracks the presentation/render and the underlying motion provenance separately.

## No blanket media license yet

The repository does not currently grant one blanket license covering every motion clip. Rights information should be recorded per catalog entry so clips with different provenance can be handled correctly.

## Provenance record

A catalog entry may record:

- archive capture creator
- motion provenance class
- source/provider name when applicable
- source reference or internal note when available
- rights/status note
- publication status

The person who captured an archive clip is not necessarily the author of the underlying motion, so those concepts should remain separate.

## Reports and removals

Ownership, provenance, safety, or other rights concerns can be reported through the repository issue form.

A disputed clip may be unpublished while it is reviewed. If a clip is removed, its catalog ID may be retained as a non-public tombstone so the archive keeps a stable record of what happened without continuing to distribute the media.
